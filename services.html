Worth asking on the call

Useful questions to have ready when you ring, so the first call gets you a clear answer.

  1. Is this work you take on?Name the vehicle's year, make and model, and describe the symptom rather than your diagnosis of it.
  2. What will you charge to look at it?Diagnosis is often billed separately from the repair. Ask whether it is, and whether it comes off the bill if you go ahead.
  3. Will I get a figure before work starts?Ask how you will be told the cost, and whether anything will be done without your go-ahead.
  4. When can you take the car, and how long will you need it?Ask before you drive over, so the trip is not wasted.
  5. Is anything guaranteed afterwards?Warranty terms on parts and labour differ from shop to shop, so it is worth asking.

Have this to hand

  • Year, make and model
  • Roughly how many miles on it
  • What the car is doing, and when it does it
  • Any warning lights that are on
  • How long you can be without the car

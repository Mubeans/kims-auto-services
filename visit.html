Have this ready when you call

Five things that let the shop give you a straight answer on the first call.

  1. The vehicleYear, make, model, and roughly how many miles are on it.
  2. The symptom, not the diagnosisWhat it does, when it does it — cold start, braking, over 40 mph — and how long it has been doing it.
  3. Warning lightsWhich ones are on, and whether they stay on or come and go.
  4. Your windowHow long you can leave the car, and whether you need it back the same day.
  5. How to reach youThe number to call once there is a figure for the work.
